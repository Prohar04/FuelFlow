# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

FuelFlow is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all community members, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone and expect all participants to uphold these standards.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Be Respectful**: Treat all community members with dignity and respect
- **Be Inclusive**: Welcome people of all backgrounds and experiences
- **Be Professional**: Maintain professional communication in all interactions
- **Be Constructive**: Provide helpful, constructive feedback
- **Be Patient**: Understand that people have different levels of knowledge
- **Be Collaborative**: Work together towards common goals
- **Be Honest**: Be truthful in your communications

### Examples of Encouraged Behavior

✅ Using welcoming and inclusive language
✅ Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
✅ Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
✅ Focusing on what is best for the community
✅ Showing empathy towards other community members
✅ Helping others learn and grow
✅ Celebrating others' contributions and achievements

### Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

❌ Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
❌ Offensive comments related to personal characteristics
❌ Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
❌ Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
❌ Publishing others' private information without permission
❌ Threats or violent language
❌ Spam or commercial solicitation
❌ Any form of bullying or abuse

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- GitHub repositories and issues
- Pull request discussions
- Email communications
- Code comments
- Social media channels
- In-person events or meetings

---

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **Email**: conduct@fuelflow.com
2. **Private Message**: Contact a maintainer directly
3. **GitHub Report**: Use the report feature on comments/issues

### What to Include

Please provide:
- Description of the incident
- People involved
- Date and location
- Any relevant links or context
- Your contact information

All reports will be:
- ✅ Treated confidentially
- ✅ Investigated promptly
- ✅ Handled fairly
- ✅ Kept anonymous if requested

---

## Enforcement

### Investigation Process

1. Report is received and acknowledged within 48 hours
2. Investigation is conducted (usually within 7 days)
3. Appropriate action is determined
4. Reporter and violator are informed of outcome

### Possible Actions

Depending on severity:

- **Warning**: Formal notice about behavior
- **Temporary Ban**: Suspension from community for 30-90 days
- **Permanent Ban**: Removal from community and repositories
- **Legal Action**: For serious violations

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers will:

- ✅ Model expected behavior
- ✅ Respond promptly to violations
- ✅ Enforce this code consistently
- ✅ Maintain confidentiality
- ✅ Recuse themselves if personally involved
- ✅ Make decisions fairly and impartially
